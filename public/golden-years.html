Ojusvi GoldenYears

A session designed for retired and retiring leaders — practical guidance to protect what you have built and give your family peace of mind.

What the session covers

  • Digital Safety

    Recognising common scams, keeping banking and UPI safe, and protecting your phone and accounts.

  • Will & Nominees

    Why a clear will matters, and how to keep nominees up to date across your accounts and investments.

  • Legacy Planning for Virtual Assets

    Planning what happens to your online accounts, digital documents and other virtual assets.

  • Benefits for Senior Citizens

    An overview of the benefits and concessions available to senior citizens, and how to make use of them.

  • Financial Awareness

    Thinking clearly about money after retirement — regular income, safety of savings and avoiding costly mistakes.

  • Family Vault

    Keeping important documents and information organised, so your family can find what they need when it matters.
